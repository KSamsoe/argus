# The Argus name

Argus's code is open source under the Apache License 2.0: anyone may use it, change it, sell what they make with it, and share their own version. The license covers the code. It does not cover the names "Argus" and "Argus Engine", or the Argus logo. This page says how you may use those.

The aim is simple: when someone sees the Argus name, it should mean this project, so nobody is misled about who made what.

## Without asking

- **Say your game is made with Argus.** "Made with Argus", "Built on the Argus Engine", on a store page, a splash screen, or credits.
- **Talk about Argus.** Articles, videos, tutorials, courses, and books may use the name to say what they are about.
- **Name your own tools and plugins for what they work with.** "Tiled importer for Argus" is fine. "Argus Tiled" makes it sound official, and is not.
- **Ship Argus unchanged,** or with only the changes a platform needs to run it, under its own name.

## Not without asking

- **A changed version under the Argus name.** If you change the engine and share or sell it, give it a name of your own. You may say it is "based on Argus" or "a fork of Argus".
- **A name or logo that could be mistaken for Argus's,** for an engine, a tool, a service, or a company.
- **Suggesting Argus endorses or partners with you** when it does not.
- **Selling the name itself:** merchandise, or a hosted service called "Argus", for example.

To ask, write to samsoekevin@gmail.com.

## Keep the credit

The Apache License already asks anyone who shares Argus, changed or not, to keep its copyright notice, the `LICENSE` file, and the `NOTICE` file, and to say what they changed. Removing them and presenting Argus as your own work is not allowed by the license, whatever you name it.
