The Argus example games

Small games made with Argus, a 2D game engine you build games in together with an AI agent. Each runs here in your browser, the same game, tick for tick, as its desktop build.

Hollow

A cave platformer with 2D lighting, dialogue, music, and rooms that stream in as you go.

Move A/D or arrows · Jump Space · Talk E · Pause Esc

Play Hollow

Delve

A turn-based dungeon on a tilemap, a new floor from the seed each time down the stairs.

Move, attack arrows · Wait . · Potion Q · Stairs Enter

Play Delve

Outpost

A colony sim: colonists find work, farm, cut wood, cook, and sleep through a day and night.

Pan WASD · Zoom - and = · Speed 1, 2, 3 · Pause Space

Play Outpost

Swarm

An arena shooter against waves of hundreds, its chase in a Rust plugin.

A browser cannot load Swarm's Rust plugin, so it plays on the desktop only, from a release's example games.

Each game's Esc menu has settings (text size, high contrast, full screen) and the controls, where a key can be rebound.

Argus draws with WebGPU where the browser has it, and WebGL2 where it does not.
