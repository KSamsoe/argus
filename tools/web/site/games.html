The example games.

02Made with Argus

A whole game, with your AI as game master.

  1. Bring Your Own Game Master

    On its own site

    A roguelike where your own AI is the game master. You type what your character does, and your AI decides what happens, as a game master does at a table.

    A small rules kernel rolls a d20 and keeps the hit points, so the game master can be inventive but can't cheat. Six floors, a boss at the bottom, and a story of your run when it ends. It runs on your own model through BYOLLM; the demo floor needs no AI at all.

    Play it Try the demo floor

03On the desktop

One that needs a desktop.

  1. Swarm

    Desktop only

    An arena shooter against waves of hundreds of enemies.

    Its chase runs in a Rust plugin, for speed, and a browser cannot load one. So Swarm plays on Windows, Linux and the Mac, from the example games that come with each release.

04Good to know

Good to know

  • Click the game first

    A game hears your keys once you click it. On a phone or a tablet, play by touch.

  • Settings in every game

    Each game's Esc menu has text size, high contrast and full screen, and its controls, where any key can be rebound.

  • Drawn on your graphics card

    The games draw with WebGPU where the browser has it, and with WebGL2 where it does not.
