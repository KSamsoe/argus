## Known limitations, stated so they are decisions

- A grid is checked against its sources once a frame and after any spawn, despawn, or component added or removed. A `NavCost` field changed within a frame (a door's cost set in one system and a path asked for in the next) shows from the next frame. The protocol's `nav.*` calls always check.
- Agents walk from cell centre to cell centre; there is no smoothing of the path, and agents do not avoid each other. Delve's monsters keep their own rule that two do not share a cell.
- An agent that is a child entity is steered (its `NavAgent` is written) but not moved: its parent moves it.
- A start cell may be blocked (an entity in a wall walks out of it); a goal cell may not.
- The costs are one byte a cell; a `NavCost` box sets the cells whose centres are inside it, the far edges not included.
- Right-angle grids only. Platformer navigation (jumps between ledges) is not this; Hollow does not use it.
