Every statement in the design document (v0.3) that constrains timelines (§13 "Tweens and sequencing", §12 camera cuts, §19's open question on the Luau heap, §6 determinism, §4 data tables), with where it lives and the test that pins it. ## §13 Tweens and sequencing

| Spec | Status | Where | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Above tweens sits a timeline: a def table of tracks ... against time." | Done | `table.rs`: the engine declares the `timeline` def table (prefix `timeline`, key `id`), so every project has one and `defs/timeline.toml` is its rows. A row without `timeline` is a timeline (its `length`, or its last cue's end); a row with `timeline = "timeline:outro"` is a cue on it: `at` seconds in, a `kind`, on a `track` (the lane an editor draws it in; its kind when left out), about `who` (`actor`, `other`, `camera`, the one drawing, or an authored `@id`). `Timelines::from_defs` reads the rows once, sorted by time (rows at one time in file order), with what each kind needs checked and the fields they name checked against the components there are (`Timelines::check`). | `timeline::a_timeline_fires_its_cues_at_their_times_and_ends`, `timeline::rows_that_do_not_read_are_said_and_a_missing_timeline_ends` |
| Tracks of tweens. | Done | A `tween` cue starts an `argus_tween::Tween` of who's field (`path`, `to`, `from` or where it is, `duration`, `easing`), marked with the `TimelinePart` that started it; the tween system plays it as any tween. | `timeline::a_timeline_fires_its_cues_at_their_times_and_ends` (the hero slides; half way it is near the middle) |
| Animator states. | Done | An `anim` cue sets who's `Animator.play` to its `state`; the animator enters it the same tick (the timeline runs first in the late stage, before `anim.update`). | `timeline::a_timeline_fires_its_cues_at_their_times_and_ends` |
| Camera cuts. | Done | A `camera` cue makes who, a camera, draw (`Camera.active`) for its `duration`, or until the timeline ends, then leaves it as active as it was; the span is a `TimelineCut` entity. The cameras' own `blend` (argus-camera, this item) says whether the cut glides and for how long, in and back. | `timeline::a_timeline_fires_its_cues_at_their_times_and_ends` (the wide camera draws, then the main one blends back); Hollow's `tests/outro.luau`, Outpost's `tests/vignette.luau` |
| Dialogue lines. | Done | A `line` cue starts a conversation (`argus_dialogue::Talk`) at its dialogue node, who speaking to the actor (or the other, when who is the actor); it plays in the world's language through the UI like any. | `timeline::a_cue_that_waits_holds_the_timeline`; Hollow's outro (the hermit's farewell) |
| Events. | Done | An `event` cue makes `effects`, the dialogue's small language (`path = value; path += n; emit name`) with the parties `actor`, `other`, and `camera`; an edit that cannot be made is a `timeline.problem` event. Every cue fired is a `timeline.cue` event (`passed: true` when its `when` failed), so the log reads as the timeline played. | `timeline::a_timeline_fires_its_cues_at_their_times_and_ends` (the bell, and a cue whose `when` fails) |
| "Playable from Luau." | Done | A timeline playing is a `Timeline` entity: a script spawns one with its `name`, `actor`, and `other` (`q:spawn({"Timeline"})`), and may set `speed`, `paused`, `looping`, or `skip`. `timeline.update` emits `timeline.started`, `timeline.cue`, `timeline.held`, `timeline.resumed`, `timeline.looped`, `timeline.ended`, and despawns it at its end. | Hollow's `systems/run.luau` and Outpost's `systems/vignette.luau` |
| A cue that waits. | Done | `wait` holds the timeline until the cue is done: its conversation ended, its tween finished, its animator state played through; on an event, until its `when` holds (a gate). While held its time stands still. | `timeline::a_cue_that_waits_holds_the_timeline`; Hollow's outro waits for the farewell to be read |
| A cutscene can be skipped. | Done | `Timeline.skip`: what it started finishes (tweens to their ends, conversations stopped with `Talk.stop`), the cues left are done at once (tweens to their ends, animator states entered, edits made; lines are not said, cuts would be over), then `timeline.ended {skipped: true}`. | `timeline::a_skip_jumps_to_the_end`; Hollow's `tests/outro.luau` (R skips the outro) |
| "Scrubbable in the editor." | Done | The editor is phase 4; the scrub is the protocol's `timeline.seek {timeline or name, time}` (argus-daemon): the run replayed from the keyframe before the timeline began to the first frame at which it had reached the time. Its `time` means every cue at or before it has fired, so the frame a scrub lands on shows them. | `argus-daemon` `timeline::a_timeline_is_listed_played_and_scrubbed`; Hollow's outro scrubbed to 1.5 s, 3 s, and 0 |
| "Cutscenes, boss intros, and Outpost's event vignettes are timelines." | Done | Hollow's outro (the way out: a wide camera glides in and pulls back over the hall, the hermit's farewell, which knows about the gem, and the hero's camera glides home) and Outpost's `lost` vignette (a colonist lost: the view glides to their grave, closes in, and glides back). No game here has a boss; a boss intro is the same table. | Hollow's `tests/outro.luau`, Outpost's `tests/vignette.luau` |

## §19, §12, §6, §4

| Spec | Status | Where | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| §19: "Timelines take sequencing and dialogue out of the heap": "sequencing lives in data, not in the Luau heap." | Done | Everything a timeline is doing is in the world: its `Timeline` (time, next cue, what it waits for, when it began), its cuts (`TimelineCut`), what it started (`TimelinePart`). A seek or a load plays on from where it was; no script keeps a coroutine. | The scrubs in Hollow's outro, with the replay checked against the run (`diverged` null) |
| §12: cutting between two cameras is an event; blends and camera moves on a curve were left for tweens and timelines. | Done | A cut is `camera.cut` (with `blend` when it glides); a blend is the camera's `blend` field; a move on a curve is a tween cue with an easing (Hollow's outro pulls its camera back along `in_out_sine`). | `argus-camera` `a_camera_with_a_blend_glides_from_the_view_before` |
| §6: the same inputs give the same world. | Done | Time is sim time times `speed`; cues fire in time order, then file order; a cue at `at` fires once the time is within 1e-4 s of it, and a tween within 1e-4 s of its end has ended (sixty steps of 1/60 in f32 are just under 1), so a tween ending at a cue's time ends the tick before it. | `argus-daemon` `timeline::a_timeline_is_listed_played_and_scrubbed` (a flare at the end of a glow is not overwritten by it) |
| §4: tables are data the agent reads and edits. | Done | `world.defs {table: "timeline"}` lists the rows; `timeline.list` shows each timeline by track in time order and what is playing; rows that do not read are `script.errors` rows (phase `timeline`) with their file and line. | `argus-daemon` `timeline::a_timeline_is_listed_played_and_scrubbed` |

## Known limitations, stated so they are decisions

- A timeline's parties are two entities (`actor`, `other`) and the drawing camera; a cue about a third names it by authored id.
- A skipped line is not said, so its conversation's edits are not made; a game that needs them makes them in an event cue.
- An event's edits set numbers, booleans, and words; a vec2 field (a position) is moved by a tween cue, not set.
- The camera blend eases in and out by a fixed curve (smoothstep); its length is the camera's.
- The editor's scrubber is phase 4; `timeline.seek` goes over frames already played, as `sim.seek` does.
